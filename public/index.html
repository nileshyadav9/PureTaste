Meet Chef David

Cooking since he was eight

Chef David started cooking at 8 years old and stepped into his first professional kitchen at 16.

More than three decades later, his journey has taken him through restaurants, hotels, catering and healthcare, and on to leading a professional kitchen as an Executive Chef. Along the way he has won culinary competitions and led live cooking demonstrations, sharing techniques and connecting with people over food.

Today he brings that experience to your table with personal chef service, customized weekly meals and catering.
